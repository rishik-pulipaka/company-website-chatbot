These terms govern the text (SMS) messages sent by Pontis Services on behalf of the local businesses that use our website chat and lead-capture tools.

Program description

When you submit a callback or service request through a chat widget powered by Pontis Services, you will receive one text message confirming that your request was received and that the business will contact you. These are transactional confirmation messages, not marketing or promotional messages.

Opting in

You opt in by submitting the request form on the business's website. Directly above the submit button, the form states: "By submitting, you agree to receive a text message about your request. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out." Submitting the form is your consent to receive the confirmation text for that request.

Message frequency

One message per request you submit. We do not send recurring or scheduled messages.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan. Pontis Services does not charge you for these messages.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will receive one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages unless you submit a new request.

Help

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or contact us at pontis.services@gmail.com.
