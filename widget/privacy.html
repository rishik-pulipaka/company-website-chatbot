Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Pontis Services ("we," "us") provides website chat and lead-capture tools to local home-services businesses, primarily HVAC contractors. This policy explains what information we collect through those tools and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit a callback or service request through a chat widget powered by Pontis Services, we collect the information you provide: your name, phone number, and a short description of the help you need. We also record the messages exchanged in that chat session so the business can follow up accurately.

How we use it

Your information is used solely to pass your request to the business you contacted and to send you a one-time text message confirming that your request was received. The business you contacted uses it to call or text you back about your request.

SMS / text messaging

If you submit a request, you will receive a single confirmation text message about that request. This is a transactional message, not marketing. Message frequency is one message per request submitted. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out of further texts; reply HELP for assistance.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your mobile phone number or your SMS consent with any third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Phone numbers are shared only with the specific business you contacted, and only so they can respond to your request.

Data sharing

We share your request details only with the business whose website you used to contact us. We use service providers (such as Twilio for SMS delivery and an email provider for owner notifications) strictly to operate this service; they are not permitted to use your information for any other purpose. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data retention

Request and message records are retained for as long as needed to support the business's follow-up and recordkeeping, and may be deleted on request.

Your choices

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. To request deletion of your information, contact us at the address below.
